Finding a Hidden Water Leak in Your Mastic, NY House.

Hidden leaks waste water and damage structure before anyone sees them. Here are the signs and the simple tests that find them.

By the Mastic Water Damage Pros team. Updated November 14, 2026.

Questions? 24/7 line.
(934) 223-1875.
Call Now.

A hidden leak sits behind a wall, under a floor or above a ceiling, and it can run for weeks before a stain appears. The first sign is often a higher water bill or a smell, not a puddle.

This guide explains the warning signs, the water meter test, checks you can do at home and when to call a plumber or a restoration team to find and dry the damage. Use it as a checklist before you open any walls.

Warning Signs.

Sign.Possible source.What to check.
Water bill rises with no change in habits.Leak in a supply line, toilet or irrigation.Meter test, toilet dye test.
Sound of running water when nothing is on.Pressurized line leaking.Listen at walls and near the meter.
Stains on ceilings or walls.Pipe, roof or fixture above.Moisture meter, check upstairs.
Musty smell.Moisture in a wall or floor.Look for damp spots and stains.
Warm spots on floors.Hot water line under slab.Plumber with detection equipment.

Mastic local note.

If your home is supplied by the Suffolk County Water Authority, your meter is the best place to start. The SCWA lists Mastic among the communities it serves.

The Town of Brookhaven Division of Building administers the building code. Ask them whether plumbing repairs require a permit.

The Water Meter Test.

Turn off every fixture and appliance that uses water, including the ice maker and irrigation. Find your water meter and look at the leak indicator or the dial. If it moves while nothing is running, water is going somewhere.

Note the reading, wait an hour without using water and read it again. A change means a leak between the meter and your fixtures. Then close the main valve inside the house and watch the meter. If it still moves, the leak is outside the house.

Simple Checks at Home.

1

Toilets.

Add a few drops of food coloring to the tank. If color shows in the bowl without flushing, the flapper leaks.

2

Under sinks.

Check cabinets for dampness, rust or swelling.

3

Appliances.

Look behind the washer, dishwasher and refrigerator for moisture.

4

Walls and ceilings.

Look for stains, bubbling paint or soft spots.

When To Call for Help.

If the meter moves and you cannot find the cause, call a licensed plumber with leak detection equipment. They can pressure test lines and listen for leaks in walls and slabs.

If you find damage, a restoration company uses moisture meters and thermal cameras to map the wet area. See slab leak water damage for under floor leaks and wall and drywall water damage repair for leaks in walls.

Preventing Hidden Leaks.

Replace old hoses, check shutoff valves each year and install water leak sensors under sinks, water heaters and appliances. Know where your main shutoff is, and turn off water when you leave for long trips.

Compare your water bills year over year. An unexplained rise is worth investigating.

Reading a Water Bill.

Compare your current bill with the same month last year, since use varies by season. A jump that does not match a change in your household suggests a leak. Look at daily usage if your water provider offers it online.

A toilet that runs silently can waste a large amount of water over a month. Irrigation systems with a broken head do the same. The meter test tells you whether water is leaving the house even when no one is using it.

Using Thermal Imaging and Moisture Meters.

A thermal camera shows temperature differences on a surface. Wet areas often look cooler because evaporation draws heat. It cannot see inside a wall, but it shows where to look with a meter.

A moisture meter confirms what the camera suggests. Together they let a technician map a leak without cutting open a wall. If the readings are high, a small opening can show the pipe and the damage.

When the Leak Is Outside the House.

If the meter moves after the main valve inside the house is closed, the leak is between the meter and the shutoff. That is often a buried service line. A leak there may show as a wet patch of lawn, a soft spot in the yard or a drop in pressure.

Repairs to the service line are done by a plumber or utility contractor. Ask your water provider who is responsible for which part of the line.

Standards and Sources.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration, and it defines water categories 1 to 3 by how contaminated the source is. We cite it as a reference for finding a hidden water leak and not as a claim about any certification. The EPA advises drying water damaged materials within 24 to 48 hours to limit mold growth.

If You Find Damage.

Once a leak is found and fixed, drying matters. Burst pipe water damage explains cleanup, and structural drying explains how wet materials are dried.
