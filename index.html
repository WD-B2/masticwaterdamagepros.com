What we do

Water Damage Restoration in Mastic Starts With Getting Water Out.

Water damage restoration in Mastic means removing the water, drying the structure and repairing what could not be saved. Most jobs begin with water extraction so that pumps and wet vacuums clear standing water before it soaks into padding, drywall and subfloor.

Drying comes next. Air movers and dehumidifiers pull moisture out of framing and finished surfaces, and a moisture meter confirms when readings are back to normal. This stage is covered in detail on our structural drying and dehumidification page.

Repairs follow once the structure is dry. That can mean replacing a section of drywall, lifting a ruined floor or rebuilding a wall cavity. Every step is explained before work starts, so you know what is being removed and why.

Mitigation and repair

Water Mitigation and Water Damage Repair in Mastic.

Water mitigation is the work done in the first hours and days to stop damage from growing. That includes shutting off the source, extracting water, setting drying equipment and removing materials that cannot dry in place.

Water damage repair in Mastic comes after mitigation. Once the structure is dry, damaged drywall, flooring, trim and paint are rebuilt so the room is usable again. Separating the two stages keeps the scope clear, and you can see what belongs to each.

If a loss involves mold, the work follows New York rules. The state Department of Labor says projects over 10 square feet need a licensed assessor and a separate licensed remediator, so we direct those jobs to our mold remediation page for the full picture.
