Manor Park is an informal name for the northernmost part of Mastic, between Sunrise Highway and Moriches-Middle Island Road, just east of Brookhaven Airport. It is not a separate municipality, and its boundaries are not officially defined.

Part of the area lies in the hamlet of Shirley, but it is served by Mastic's ZIP code, 11950. Real estate listings also use Manor Park as a subdivision name, which points to everyday local use of the label.

Calls from Manor Park go to the same 24/7 line as the rest of Mastic. Tell us the street, where the water is and what caused it.

Locating Manor Park.

Grove Drive is a useful reference point. North of it, the Eastport-South Manor Central School District serves the area, while most of Mastic is in the William Floyd district.

Listings we reviewed describe Cape, ranch and high ranch homes. Mastic as a whole had 4,792 housing units in the 2020 Census, and no separate count exists for Manor Park.

Why Most Calls Here Start Inside the House.

This page does not claim that Manor Park floods. Documented storm flooding in the Mastic area is concentrated near Moriches Bay and the Forge River, well south of this section.

Water damage here is more likely to begin with a burst pipe, an appliance leak or a water heater leak. These are plumbing events that can happen in any home, and the first hour decides how far the water spreads.

If Water Is Still Spreading in Manor Park.

Stop the source.

Close the main valve or the fixture shutoff if you can reach it safely. If you cannot find it, call and we will help.

Keep clear of electrical hazards.

Do not stand in water near outlets, cords or the panel. Call the utility or emergency services for immediate dangers.

Lift valuables off the floor.

Move rugs, shoes, papers and small electronics to a dry shelf when it is safe to do so.

Photograph the damage.

Take wide and close photos before anything is moved. They help when you talk to your insurer.

How Drying Works After the Water Is Out.

After water extraction, air movers and dehumidifiers dry framing, subfloor and wall cavities. Readings are logged each visit so you can see progress.

A wall that looks dry can still hold moisture. For that reason technicians open small sections when readings stay high, as wall and drywall repair describes.

Insurance Documentation.

Photos of the damage, a list of affected items and the moisture log help your insurer understand the loss. Coverage depends on the cause and on your policy terms.

We document the work and your insurer decides coverage. The New York Department of Financial Services says a denial must cite the policy language.

Reference Standards.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration, and it defines water categories 1 to 3 by how contaminated the source is. We cite it as a reference for water damage in Manor Park and not as a claim about any certification. The EPA advises drying water damaged materials within 24 to 48 hours to limit mold growth.
