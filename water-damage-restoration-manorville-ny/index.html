Wetlands, Moraine and Ponds.

The hamlet lies in the Core Preservation Area of the Central Pine Barrens, partly on the Ronkonkoma terminal moraine. Kettle hole ponds such as Punk's Hole and wetlands near the Peconic River make the ground uneven, and groundwater is closer in low spots.

Long Driveways, Detached Garages and Outbuildings.

Large Manorville lots often include detached garages, workshops and sheds with their own water lines. A line that freezes in an outbuilding can run unnoticed for days because no one goes there during winter.
