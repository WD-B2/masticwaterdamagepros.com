Vacant and Seasonal Homes.

A home that sits empty for weeks can develop a burst pipe, a failed hose or a slow roof leak with no one there to see it. By the time water is found, it may have soaked floors, walls and ceilings below.

Mold Concerns After a Vacant Home Leak.

A leak in a home that sat empty gives mold time to start. Materials that stay wet for more than a day or two may grow mold, and the smell on re-entry can be the first sign. If you see growth, do not scrub or spray it dry.

Mold remediation explains how New York licensing and assessment work, and what to ask before you hire. Drying the structure promptly lowers the risk, which is why a quick call after finding a leak matters.

Keep photos of when you found the leak, what you saw and any steps you took. That record helps your insurer and ours.

Reference Standards.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration, and it defines water categories 1 to 3 by how contaminated the source is. We cite it as a reference for water damage in East Moriches and not as a claim about any certification. The EPA advises drying water damaged materials within 24 to 48 hours to limit mold growth.
